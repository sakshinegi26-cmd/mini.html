    • About Maggi

    Maggi is a popular and tasty instant noodle dish. It is easy and quick to prepare. We can add vegetables to make it more delicious.

    Ingredients

    • 1 packet of Maggi noodles
    • 1 cup of water
    • 1 Maggi tastemaker
    • 1 small onion (optional)
    • 1 small tomato (optional)
    • Some green peas (optional)

    Instructions

    1. Take a pan and add 1 cup of water.
    2. Heat the water until it starts boiling.
    3. Add the Maggi noodles to the water.
    4. Add the tastemaker and vegetables.
    5. Cook for a few minutes until the noodles are soft.
    6. Serve the hot Maggi in a bowl.

    Important Tip

    Tip: Add vegetables to make Maggi more nutritious. Enjoy your delicious Maggi!
